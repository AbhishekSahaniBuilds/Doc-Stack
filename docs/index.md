**Doc-Stack** is the central place for documentation, guides, development notes, technical resources, and information about my software and hardware projects.

## <u>About the Developer</u>

Hello, I am Abhishek Sahani. I'm a learning developer interested in building practical tools and exploring how technology can turn ideas into reality.

I build independent, open-source projects with a focus on **speed, accessibility, simplicity, and functionality** rather than unnecessary complexity. I want to make useful projects freely available to everyone while continuing to learn and experiment, particularly in AI and Machine Learning.

**My philosophy:** <i>"Hard things are easy until your mind makes them difficult."</i>
